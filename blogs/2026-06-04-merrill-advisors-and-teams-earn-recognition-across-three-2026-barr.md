Merrill achieved significant recognition in Barron's 2026 rankings, with 126 advisors named to the Top 250 Private Wealth Management Teams list, 20 advisors on the Top 100 Financial Advisors list, and 24 institutional consultants recognized on the Top 100 Institutional Consulting Teams list. The recognition reflects advisors' experience, regulatory compliance, client satisfaction, and community involvement.
