Bank of America announced the appointment of Chip McLeod, a Merrill Lynch Wealth Management senior resident director with over three decades of financial services experience, as the new president of Upstate South Carolina. McLeod succeeds Stacy Brandon, who retired after 39 years with the company, and will lead efforts to expand market share across the five-county Upstate region while overseeing Bank of America's eight lines of business.
