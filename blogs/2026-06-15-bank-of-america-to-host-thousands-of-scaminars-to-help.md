Bank of America announced plans to conduct 2,500 in-person scam and fraud prevention seminars by year-end 2026, reaching tens of thousands of consumers. Tied to Elder Abuse Awareness Month, the initiative uses curriculum from Bank of America's Better Money Habits platform to help attendees identify scams and access resources.
