Bank of America announced a new cross-border real-time payments solution launching next quarter that will enable corporate and financial institution clients to send and receive funds instantly via Swift or CashPro. The service supports high-volume transactions like international remittances and gig-worker payouts with real-time tracking, full principal preservation, and reduced costs aligned with G20 payment objectives.
