Bank of America and the Kansas City Current renewed their multi-year partnership to expand community soccer initiatives. The organizations will collaborate on free "Soccer With Us" clinics for underserved youth, and Bank of America will secure an entitlement night at an upcoming 2026 home match at CPKC Stadium.
